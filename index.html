The Vessel

Less storage space
than two jet-skis.

The NEMO rewrote what private submarine ownership requires. At 2,500 kg and 1.55 m tall, it goes where no submersible could go before.

At the heart of the NEMO is a state-of-the-art acrylic pressure hull — a completely transparent sphere that hides nothing from view. The surrounding structure was designed around that single idea: an unobstructed window into the ocean, with none of the ballast tanks, batteries or framework that clutter the view from a conventional submersible.

Four silent electric thrusters let the NEMO hover beside an object of interest or hold station against a current while descending. Two intelligent control systems govern it. The MANTA controller offers pinpoint manoeuvring and hands steering to the passenger under pilot supervision. The MARLIN is a wireless surface remote — it can move the submarine away from the support vessel with nobody aboard, or hold it in position over a dive site during passenger transfer.

Because of its weight and single lifting point, the NEMO can be set down on any flat surface without a cradle or davit. It deploys from a yacht, a boat ramp, a beach launcher, or a towable trailer — which makes ownership realistic for people who do not own a superyacht.

Safety & Systems

Engineered to surface

Every protection system on the NEMO is designed around a single assumption: that the pilot may be unable to act.

Protection

Dead Man Switch

Below 5 msw a timer runs continuously. At nine minutes the pilot is prompted; at ten an alarm sounds; ten seconds later the diving tanks blow automatically and upward propulsion engages. The vessel surfaces whether or not anyone is conscious aboard.

Protection

Maximum Depth Protection

A warning is raised 5 m above the configured limit. At the limit an audible alarm sounds. Five metres beyond it, the tanks blow and upward thrust engages automatically until the sub is back within its envelope.

Control

MANTA & MARLIN

MANTA gives pinpoint manoeuvring with auto-heading and auto-depth, and hands supervised control to the passenger. MARLIN is a wireless surface remote for launch, recovery, and unmanned positioning over a dive site.

Emergency

Drop weight & release buoy

A 100 kg drop weight can be released manually from inside on an independent 60-bar emergency hydraulic circuit — the same circuit that serves the buoy and manipulator releases.

Atmosphere

Redundant monitoring

Two completely independent Dräger X-am 5600 units track O₂, CO₂ and H₂, backed by a dedicated Freon sensor for the air-conditioning circuit. Alarms trigger well inside safe limits.

Navigation

Comms & positioning

Underwater telephone, VHF, sonar, USBL positioning, altimeter, external LED arrays and a top light. An optional manipulator arm can be fitted for sampling and recovery work.

The International SeaKeepers Society

South Pacific Chapter

Established in 2023, the SeaKeepers South Pacific Chapter connects yachts and boats with marine research initiatives across one of the world's most vital maritime regions. Through the DISCOVERY Programme, private boat and yacht owners contribute to oceanographic research, citizen science, conservation and sustainability efforts throughout the South Pacific.

The chapter operates through registered charitable trusts in both New Zealand and Australia. The Australian Charitable Trust holds Deductible Gift Recipient (DGR) status, enabling eligible donors to claim tax benefits on their contributions.

A submersible like the NEMO extends what a DISCOVERY vessel can contribute — carrying scientists to depths that scuba cannot safely reach, and bringing back observation and imagery from the mesophotic zone.
